## The request and the problems found

The user suspected three things:
- retrieval returns papers that are not relevant;
- retrieved papers are not used well, and prompts may narrow the model's reasoning;
- a loop of many hypotheses, multi-model critiques, re-retrieval and revision would do better.

**The retrieval suspicion was right.**
- **How the old queries work.** Each old query is an OR of single words (for example `density OR reduction`) inside
  the high-entropy-alloy domain. Candidates are ranked by word overlap with the question, template words included.
- **How much is off-topic.** The same relevance screener grades only **36%** of the old corpus's 8,950 papers as
  relevant to their question; 21% are unrelated.
- **An example.** The density question on HfMoTaTiZr had a magnetocaloric paper at rank 4.

## The arms (o3-mini writer; 270 runs each)

| Arm | Focused retrieval (R) | Literature map + broad prompt (U) | Critique loop (C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rx_base` | – | – | – |
| `rx_r` | ✓ | – | – |
| `rx_u` | – | ✓ | – |
| `rx_ru` | ✓ | ✓ | – |
| `rx_c` | – | – | loop |
| `rx_ruc_select` | ✓ | ✓ | one critique round, select |
| `rx_ruc` | ✓ | ✓ | loop |

- **Focused retrieval.** o3-mini plans concept searches. gpt-4o screens titles and abstracts for relevance and grades
  passages. The old corpus is kept as a second channel.
- **The loop.**
  - o3-mini writes 4 candidates.
  - o1, gpt-4o and Llama-3.3 critique them, blind and shuffled.
  - The reviewers' doubts drive a new search (at most 8,000 characters).
  - o3-mini revises.
  - It repeats until a majority of critics is satisfied, at most 3 rounds.
- **Same evidence ceiling.** Every arm sees at most 64,000 distinct characters.

## Interim results

DeepSeek decides until GPT-5.6 is complete. 187 of 1,890 GPT-5.6 judgments were done when this was written.

| Arm | Reconstruction (DeepSeek) | vs base [CI] | Quality, pairwise vs base |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `rx_base` | 0.630 | – | – |
| **`rx_r`** | **0.662** | +0.032 [−0.05, +0.11] | **+0.16 [+0.09, +0.22], 26/5 papers** |
| `rx_u` | 0.569 | −0.060 [−0.13, +0.01] | +0.07 (not after Holm) |
| `rx_ru` | 0.554 | −0.076 [−0.17, +0.01] | – |
| `rx_c` | 0.480 | **−0.150 [−0.23, −0.07]** | **−0.27 [−0.35, −0.19], 5/38** |
| `rx_ruc_select` | 0.447 | – | – |
| `rx_ruc` | 0.402 | **−0.228 [−0.31, −0.15]** | −0.04 |

- **Focused retrieval works.**
  - Every selected paper is relevant (35% "directly on point"), and 85% of packed passages are graded fully relevant.
  - Hypotheses written from them are preferred without the answer key in 26 of 45 papers.
  - Reconstruction is +0.03, not detectable.
  - The paper's specific lever is still almost never in the earlier literature (1.6% of passages, DeepSeek labels).
- **The broad prompt and literature map do not help** (−0.06).
- **The critique loop makes hypotheses worse.**
  - Four candidates written in one call are shorter and less specific (230–265 words against 346–381).
  - Critic selection among them lowers the score.
  - Revision with critique-driven evidence helps within the loop (+0.19 pairwise over select-only), but does not
    recover the loss.
  - The critics agree only weakly with each other (Spearman 0.29–0.43). They also disagree with the independent
    judge: they call 88% of final hypotheses fine.
- **Research quality (three DeepSeek calls)** hardly separates the arms (2.86–3.06).
- **Leakage: none detected.** All counters zero; 80,198 seen-record reads re-dated, 0 violations.
