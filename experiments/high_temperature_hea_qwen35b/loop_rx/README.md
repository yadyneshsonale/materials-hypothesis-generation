## The request and the problems found

The user suspected three things:
- retrieval returns papers that are not relevant;
- retrieved papers are not used well, and prompts may narrow the model's reasoning;
- a loop of many hypotheses, multi-model critiques, re-retrieval and revision would do better.

**The retrieval suspicion was right.**
- **How the old queries work.** Each old query is an OR of single words (for example `density OR reduction`) inside
  the high-entropy-alloy domain. Candidates are ranked by word overlap with the question, template words included.
- **How much is off-topic.** The same relevance screener grades only **36%** of the old corpus's 8,950 papers as
  relevant to their question; 21% are unrelated.
- **An example.** The density question on HfMoTaTiZr had a magnetocaloric paper at rank 4.

## The arms (o3-mini writer; 270 runs each)

| Arm | Focused retrieval (R) | Literature map + broad prompt (U) | Critique loop (C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rx_base` | – | – | – |
| `rx_r` | ✓ | – | – |
| `rx_u` | – | ✓ | – |
| `rx_ru` | ✓ | ✓ | – |
| `rx_c` | – | – | loop |
| `rx_ruc_select` | ✓ | ✓ | one critique round, select |
| `rx_ruc` | ✓ | ✓ | loop |

- **Focused retrieval.** o3-mini plans concept searches. gpt-4o screens titles and abstracts for relevance and grades
  passages. The old corpus is kept as a second channel.
- **The loop.**
  - o3-mini writes 4 candidates.
  - o1, gpt-4o and Llama-3.3 critique them, blind and shuffled.
  - The reviewers' doubts drive a new search (at most 8,000 characters).
  - o3-mini revises.
  - It repeats until a majority of critics is satisfied, at most 3 rounds.
- **Same evidence ceiling.** Every arm sees at most 64,000 distinct characters.

## Interim results

DeepSeek decides until GPT-5.6 is complete. 187 of 1,890 GPT-5.6 judgments were done when this was written.

| Arm | Reconstruction (DeepSeek) | vs base [CI] | Quality, pairwise vs base |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `rx_base` | 0.630 | – | – |
| **`rx_r`** | **0.662** | +0.032 [−0.05, +0.11] | **+0.16 [+0.09, +0.22], 26/5 papers** |
| `rx_u` | 0.569 | −0.060 [−0.13, +0.01] | +0.07 (not after Holm) |
| `rx_ru` | 0.554 | −0.076 [−0.17, +0.01] | – |
| `rx_c` | 0.480 | **−0.150 [−0.23, −0.07]** | **−0.27 [−0.35, −0.19], 5/38** |
| `rx_ruc_select` | 0.447 | – | – |
| `rx_ruc` | 0.402 | **−0.228 [−0.31, −0.15]** | −0.04 |

- **Focused retrieval works.**
  - Every selected paper is relevant (35% "directly on point"), and 85% of packed passages are graded fully relevant.
  - Hypotheses written from them are preferred without the answer key in 26 of 45 papers.
  - Reconstruction is +0.03, not detectable.
  - The paper's specific lever is still almost never in the earlier literature (1.6% of passages, DeepSeek labels).
- **The broad prompt and literature map do not help** (−0.06).
- **The critique loop makes hypotheses worse.**
  - Four candidates written in one call are shorter and less specific (230–265 words against 346–381).
  - Critic selection among them lowers the score.
  - Revision with critique-driven evidence helps within the loop (+0.19 pairwise over select-only), but does not
    recover the loss.
  - The critics agree only weakly with each other (Spearman 0.29–0.43). They also disagree with the independent
    judge: they call 88% of final hypotheses fine.
- **Research quality (three DeepSeek calls)** hardly separates the arms (2.86–3.06).
- **Leakage: none detected.** All counters zero; 80,198 seen-record reads re-dated, 0 violations.

## Experiment 45: keep the writer's lever, fix how the hypothesis is written, state the domain

**Plan and results.** [loop.md](../../../loop.md) section 26: 26.1 failure analysis and pre-registration; 26.2

**Code.**
- [edit_loop.py](../../../edit_loop.py): the arms (`edit_*`, `domain_*`).
- [edit_experiment.py](../../../edit_experiment.py): runner and analysis.
- Tests: [test_edit_loop.py](../../../test_edit_loop.py).

**Runners** (in [loop/](../loop/README.md)):
- `run_x45.sh`: generation;
- `run_x45_eval.sh`: DeepSeek, RQ ×3, pairwise (DeepSeek, o1) and report;
- `run_x45_gpt.sh`: GPT-5.6 judge and audits on both instances. It replaced `run_rx_gpt2.sh` at 12:17 on 2026-11-02
  and also does experiment 44's GPT-5.6 work. It was paused at the user's request at 22:30 on 2026-11-02 (loop.md 26.5); rerunning it resumes where it stopped.

**Report.** `analysis_edit.md` and `analysis_edit.json`, regenerated by `python edit_experiment.py report`.

**Why.**
- Any instruction added to the writer's prompt moves it off its own best guess of the lever, the strongest
  target-free predictor (experiments 41–42).
- So this experiment changes only how the finished hypothesis is written (E), and adds one sentence of domain
  context where the question omits the alloy class (D).
- Both were chosen from a regression over the 15,600 judged hypotheses of earlier experiments.

**Arms** (o3-mini; experiment 44's packs).

| Arm | What | Runs |
|---|---|---:|
| `edit_rx_base`, `edit_rx_r` | E on experiment 44's `rx_base` / `rx_r` runs | 270 each |
| `domain_rx_base`, `domain_rx_r` | WRITER_B plus the research context on the 44 questions that do not name the alloy class | 88 each |
| `domain_edit_rx_base`, `domain_edit_rx_r` | E on the domain runs | 88 each |

- **E, one editor call** that keeps the lever (checked in code):
  - removes numbers that neither the question nor the cited passages state;
  - keeps the conditions to the question's regime;
  - names a multi-principal-element effect only where the mechanism acts through one;
  - states how the intervention acts.
- **Composed agents.** D is the base on questions that name the alloy class, and D on the others. D+E is E, and D+E
  on the others.

**Results.** Every evaluation is complete except the GPT-5.6 audits (paused at the user's request at 22:30 on 2026-11-02 (loop.md 26.5)).
`must_obey_check.py` passes.

| Variant | GPT-5.6 vs base [CI] | DeepSeek vs base | Pairwise quality vs base (DeepSeek) | RQ vs base |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E on `rx_base` | −0.010 [−0.04, +0.02] | +0.016 | **−0.27, 0/36 papers** | −0.01 |
| D on `rx_base` | +0.012 [−0.01, +0.03] | +0.016 | +0.01 | 0.00 |
| D+E on `rx_base` | +0.015 [−0.02, +0.04] | +0.068 [+0.01, +0.12] | **−0.26, 1/38** | −0.04 |
| E on `rx_r` | −0.004 [−0.03, +0.02] | −0.017 | **−0.30, 0/39** | −0.06 |
| D on `rx_r` | +0.006 [−0.01, +0.02] | +0.011 | +0.06 | +0.01 |
| D+E on `rx_r` | −0.004 [−0.03, +0.03] | −0.006 | **−0.25, 1/35** | −0.07 |

- **The editor did what it was asked, and it does not help.**
  - Numbers not in the sources fell from 26% to 2% of interventions and from 40% to 3% of conditions, with no
    change in length.
  - But reconstruction moves by ±0.02.
  - The judges that do not see the answer key prefer the original hypotheses in almost every paper.
- **The domain sentence barely changes the writer.** Hypotheses bringing in the alloy class went from 31% to 34% and
  from 24% to 35%. Its effects are within ±0.06.
- **Conclusion:** adopt neither.
- **Leakage: none detected.** All counters zero; 40,718 seen-record reads re-dated, 0 violations.
