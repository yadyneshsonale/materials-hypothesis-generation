## Why a second measure

The user asked for an agent that averages 2.5+.
- **On the reconstruction score, 2.5 is out of reach without leakage.**
  - Told the paper's exact lever, the writer reaches 1.26 / 1.50.
  - The best of 15,600 existing hypotheses, picked with the answer key, reaches 1.28 / 1.52.
  - Four independent reviews agree: an agent with perfect knowledge of the earlier literature would top out around
    2.0–2.25.
- **So 2.5 was pursued on a separate, target-free research-quality score (RQ).** It is defined by the expert
  package's own blind scales: plausibility, testability and usefulness, each 1–5, anchors verbatim.
  - RQ = their mean minus 1, on 0–4.
  - The judge sees what the expert sees: the question and the rendered hypothesis, with no citations, no evidence
    and no answer key.
  - RQ is never reported as reconstruction.

## Results

**Instrument checks** (216 sanity controls; re-test on 135 writer runs).
- **o1 fails.** It sits at the ceiling: testability 4.94 of 5, the Llama baseline 3.17, re-test ρ 0.16.
- **Single-call DeepSeek fails re-test** (ρ 0.64).
- **DeepSeek-3** (the mean of three calls; a post-hoc amendment) **passes all four checks**, with a measured re-test
  of ρ 0.82.

**Research quality** (DeepSeek-3; per-paper means, 95% CI).

| Configuration | RQ |
|---|---|
| Writer (`writer_only_b_o3mini`, the best method) | **3.10 [3.00, 3.20]** |
| Complete-study writer | 3.13 |
| Web writers | 3.03–3.06 |
| o3-mini, plain prompt | 2.97 |
| The authors' actual study, written by the same writer | 2.71 |
| Llama-3.3 baseline | 1.40 |

- Every o3-mini configuration is above 2.5, so the pre-registered stopping rule was met without new agents.
- The writer leads the Llama baseline by +1.70 (all 45 papers), against +0.11 on reconstruction.
- **The judge rates the writer above the authors' actual studies** (+0.39, 33 of 45 papers).
- **Convergence with the GPT-5.6 audits is weak** (ρ ≤ 0.25).

**Reading.** The agent's hypotheses are rated sound, testable and useful by an AI judge, as highly as what the
authors actually did. But AI-judged quality is a weak instrument: it is reliable only when averaged, o1 cannot
discriminate, and the judge prefers AI proposals to real studies. The expert's ratings of the same 60 items are the
decisive test. Until they arrive, "2.5+ on research quality" is provisional.
