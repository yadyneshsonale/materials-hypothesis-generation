# Experiment 46: strong research proposals as the only objective

From experiment 46 (2026-11-05) the only objective is the quality of the research proposal, judged without the
answer key. Reconstruction is retired (must_obey.md 6.2, as amended).

**Plan and results.** [loop.md](../../../loop.md) section 27: 27.1 is the decision, the instrument and the
pre-registered plan for phases 0–1.

**Code.**
- [quality_eval.py](../../../quality_eval.py): the grounding judge and its checks, pairwise preference, the
  leaderboard.
- [rq_judge.py](../../../rq_judge.py): the research-quality (RQ) judge on the expert's three blind scales.
- [must_obey_check.py](../../../must_obey_check.py) `--quality`: the integrity gate for this objective.
- Tests: [test_quality_eval.py](../../../test_quality_eval.py).

**The measure.**
- **SP, the strong-proposal score (0–4)**: the mean of four 1–5 scales minus 1.
  - Plausibility, testability and usefulness come from the RQ judge (three DeepSeek calls).
  - Grounding asks whether the key premises are supported by the cited earlier work (one DeepSeek call).
  - Grounding counts only after its checks pass; until then SP is RQ.
- **Decisions**: pairwise preference over the incumbent, both orders. DeepSeek decides during development, GPT-5.6
  confirms, o1 alongside.

**Runners** (in [loop/](../loop/README.md)):
- `run_x46.sh`: DeepSeek; phase 0 checks, then phases 1a and 1b.
- `run_x46_o1.sh`: o1 pairwise.
- `run_x46_gpt.sh`: GPT-5.6 confirmation.

**Outputs.**
- `grounding/<kind>/<configuration>/deepseek/<run>.json`: kinds `runs`, and the checks `g_retest`, `g_swap`,
  `g_removed`.
- `pairwise/<configuration>__vs__writer_only_b_o3mini/<judge>/`: both orders per question and sample.
- `analysis_quality.md` and `.json`, regenerated by `python quality_eval.py report`.
- RQ judgments stay in `../loop_rq/rq/`. This session's sanity controls are `controls_s46`; the writer's
  same-session RQ is `rerun46`.
