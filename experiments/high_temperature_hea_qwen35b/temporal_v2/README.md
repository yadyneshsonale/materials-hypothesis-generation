# Temporal hypothesis-reconstruction v2 results

This directory contains the completed chronological hypothesis-reconstruction experiment.
The workflow asked whether an agent could propose a hypothesis related to a held-out paper using
only corpus evidence published before that paper.

## Important interpretation

The public Qwen3.5-35B-A3B documentation does not provide a precise training-data cutoff.
Consequently, these results are **retrospective temporal reconstructions**, not proof that the
model had never encountered the test papers during pretraining. A strict unseen-future claim
requires a documented model cutoff and test papers published after it.

## Chronological split

- Training evidence: 38 papers published through 2023.
- Validation cases: 8 papers published in 2024.
- Test cases: 4 papers published in 2025–2026.
- Held-out cases evaluated: 12.
- No cases were excluded.

Each held-out case was evaluated with:

- three query levels: broad, problem-constrained, and expert-context;
- four representations: roles only, v1 linked evidence, normalized v2 evidence, and v2 evidence
  plus roles/actionable questions.

This produced 144 completed runs.

## V2 evidence extraction

- Papers: 50.
- Valid evidence units: 276.
- Invalid persisted units: 0.
- Exact text-grounding rate: 100%.
- Current-paper units: 185.
- Cited-work units: 91.
- Units with conditions: 99.28%.
- Units with processing lineage: 90.58%.
- Units with structure observations: 76.45%.
- Units with measurements: 66.30%.
- Units with boundaries: 36.96%.
- Units with limitations: 58.33%.
- Units with normalized composition: 53.62%.
- Units with measurement uncertainty: 11.96%.

Property vocabulary remains fragmented: 276 units contain 267 distinct property labels. This
confirms that ontology normalization remains necessary before quantitative cross-paper synthesis.

## Experiment results

Scores range from 0 to 4.

| Representation | Exact | Partial | Plausible alternative | Unsupported | Problem alignment | Intervention | Mechanism | Evidence validity | Novelty |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Roles only | 2 | 8 | 20 | 6 | 3.72 | 2.14 | 2.08 | 2.78 | 2.61 |
| V1 evidence | 3 | 10 | 17 | 6 | 3.72 | 1.89 | 1.94 | 2.83 | 2.53 |
| V2 evidence | 2 | 7 | 20 | 7 | 3.39 | 1.81 | 1.75 | 2.58 | 2.42 |
| V2 + roles + questions | 1 | 8 | 24 | 3 | 3.61 | 1.75 | 1.81 | 2.92 | 2.75 |

The redesigned representation did **not** improve exact intervention or mechanism reconstruction
in this first experiment. Roles-only retrieval had the strongest mean intervention and mechanism
agreement. Combining v2 evidence with roles and actionable questions did, however:

- halve unsupported outputs from six to three relative to roles only;
- produce the most plausible alternatives;
- achieve the highest evidence-validity score; and
- achieve the highest novelty score.

These results suggest that the normalized schema is useful for constraining and diversifying
hypotheses, but retrieval and property/composition normalization must improve before it becomes a
better reconstruction representation.

All scores were generated by the same model and require blinded materials-expert evaluation.
They must not be treated as definitive scientific quality judgments.

## Files

- `temporal_metadata.json`: exact JATS dates, split eligibility, work-family IDs, and model-cutoff
  status.
- `question_triage.json`: conservative classification of all 8,848 questions.
- `materials_evidence_v2/`: 50 normalized, exactly grounded paper records.
- `benchmark_cases/`: leakage-audited queries and hidden structured targets for 12 papers.
- `experiment_runs/`: 144 generated hypotheses, retrieved evidence, and evaluations.
- `results/v2_evidence_evaluation.json`: extraction-quality evaluation.
- `results/integrity_audit.json`: final matrix, temporal, citation, score, and query-leakage audit.
- `status.json`: final workflow state.
- `COMPLETE`: completion marker.

Transient llama.cpp logs and chunk checkpoints are intentionally excluded from version control.
The workflow is restart-safe and can recreate them.
