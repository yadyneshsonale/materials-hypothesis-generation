## What is in the sheet

| Tab | Rows | What you enter (choose from the drop-down lists) |
|---|---|---|
| **START HERE** | – | Your name or initials, your field, the date. Shows your progress (how many ratings are done). |
| **Part 1 – hypotheses** | 60 (`Q01-A` … `Q20-C`) | Plausibility, Testability, Usefulness (1–5 each) |
| **Part 1 – questions** | 20 (`Q01` … `Q20`) | Does the question make sense? (1–5); Closest to what the authors did (A, B, C, Can't tell) |
| **Part 2 – hypotheses** | 60 (`Q01-A` … `Q20-C`) | Agreement with the paper: Intervention, Conditions, Mechanism, Outcome (0–4 each) |
| **Part 2 – questions** | 20 (`Q01` … `Q20`) | Answer key accurate? (1–5); Question hints at the answer? (No, Slightly, Strongly) |

- **Yellow cells** are where you enter ratings. Click a cell and choose from the drop-down list.
- **Question names are links.** Clicking `Q01` opens that question's page in your browser (the Part 1 or Part 2 page,
  matching the tab).
- **Comments** (last column) are optional. Use them for anything worth noting: errors in a hypothesis or an answer
  key, an unclear question, or the reason for an extreme score.
- Leave a cell empty if you skip it. Please do not type in the grey label columns.

## No spreadsheet program?

The same tables are in [csv/](csv/) as four plain CSV files:
- `part_1_hypotheses.csv`
- `part_1_questions.csv`
- `part_2_hypotheses.csv`
- `part_2_questions.csv`

Fill them in any editor and send all four back. Use the same values as the drop-down lists, for example `4`, `B`,
`Can't tell` or `Slightly`.

## When you are done

Save the file as `expert_ratings_<your initials>.xlsx` and see [5_returned_ratings](../5_returned_ratings/README.md).
