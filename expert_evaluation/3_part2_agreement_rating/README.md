> **Only start Part 2 after you have finished Part 1 for all the questions you are doing.** These pages show which
> paper each question came from and what it found.

## What to do for each question (about 6 minutes)

1. Open the question page, starting with [Q01](Q01.md). Each page has:
   - **the paper:** title, journal and year, with links to the free full text;
   - **the same research question** as in Part 1;
   - **what the paper actually did and found:** an automatically made summary that we call the *answer key*;
   - **the same three hypotheses, A, B and C**, as in Part 1, with the same letters.
2. **Check the answer key.** Compare it with the paper; the abstract and conclusions are usually enough. In tab
   **Part 2 – questions**, row `Q01`:
   - **Answer key accurate? (1–5)**
   - **Question hints at the answer?** No, Slightly or Strongly. Now that you know the answer, does the question give
     it away?
3. **Rate each hypothesis's agreement with the paper.** In tab **Part 2 – hypotheses**, rows `Q01-A`, `Q01-B` and
   `Q01-C`, score each aspect from 0 to 4:
   - **Intervention:** what is changed or studied, including key parameters.
   - **Conditions:** processing, test or simulation conditions.
   - **Mechanism:** the structure through which the effect acts, and the explanation.
   - **Outcome:** the predicted effect on the property, in direction and size.

   0 means different or missing, 2 means the same kind but different specifics, and 4 means essentially what the
   paper did. The full scales are in [1_read_first](../1_read_first/README.md#part-2-scales-after-you-have-seen-the-paper).
4. Optionally, add a note in **Comments**, for example if the answer key is wrong.
5. Click **Next →** at the bottom of the page and repeat for Q02, Q03, …

Notes:
- **Judge each aspect separately.** A hypothesis can match the mechanism but not the intervention.
- **One hypothesis may closely restate the paper.** Rate it the same way as the others.
- **If the answer key is wrong,** rate agreement with the paper itself and say so in Comments.
