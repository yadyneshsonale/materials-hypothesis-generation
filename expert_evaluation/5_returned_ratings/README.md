# 5 · Sending your ratings back

[↩ Back to the start page](../README.md)

## Before you send

- [ ] Your name or initials are filled in on the **START HERE** tab.
- [ ] For every question you did, all rows in the four tabs are filled. The progress counter on **START HERE**
  shows how many ratings are done.
- [ ] Part 1 was finished before you started Part 2.
- [ ] The file is saved as `expert_ratings_<your initials>.xlsx`, for example `expert_ratings_AB.xlsx`.

## How to send it

**Option 1 (simplest):** email the file to the person who sent you this link (Yadynesh).

**Option 2 (if you have write access to this repository):** upload it to this folder:
1. Open this folder on GitHub.
2. Click **Add file → Upload files**.
3. Drag your file in.
4. Click **Commit changes**.

## What happens next

- We **unblind** your ratings: we match each hypothesis A, B or C to its hidden source.
- We compare your agreement scores with the AI judge's scores for the same 60 hypotheses. The main question is
  whether the AI judge ranks hypotheses the way you do.
- We also check, using your ratings:
  - whether the stronger AI system is better than the weaker one;
  - how plausible and useful the AI hypotheses are compared with the authors' actual study;
  - whether our automatic summaries of the papers are accurate;
  - whether the questions make sense or give away the answer.
- We will share the results with you.

Thank you very much for your time and expertise.
