# 2 · Part 1: blind rating

[↩ Back to the start page](../README.md) · [Rating scales](../1_read_first/README.md)

In Part 1 you rate the hypotheses **without knowing which paper each question comes from**. Please do not search for
the papers during Part 1.

## What to do for each question (about 7 minutes)

1. Open the question page, starting with [Q01](Q01.md). Each page has:
   - **the research question**, as the AI systems saw it;
   - **three hypotheses, A, B and C**, all written for that question. They use the same fields (main hypothesis,
     starting material, what is changed or studied, conditions, mechanism, predicted outcome, test that would
     falsify it, …).
2. Read the question, then all three hypotheses.
3. In the rating sheet, tab **Part 1 – hypotheses**, rows `Q01-A`, `Q01-B` and `Q01-C`, choose from the drop-down
   lists:
   - **Plausibility (1–5):** is it scientifically sound for this material and these conditions?
   - **Testability (1–5):** could it be clearly tested, and would the proposed test decide it?
   - **Usefulness (1–5):** would it help a researcher working on exactly this question?
4. In tab **Part 1 – questions**, row `Q01`:
   - **Does the question make sense? (1–5)**
   - **Closest to what the authors did:** A, B, C or Can't tell (your best guess).
5. Optionally, add a note in **Comments**.
6. Click **Next →** at the bottom of the page and repeat for Q02, Q03, …

The full definition of every score point is in [1_read_first](../1_read_first/README.md#part-1-scales-blind-you-have-not-seen-the-paper).

**When you have finished Part 1 for all the questions you are doing, go to
[Part 2](../3_part2_agreement_rating/README.md).** Please do not change Part 1 ratings after that.
