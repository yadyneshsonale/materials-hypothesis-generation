# Expert evaluation: AI-generated hypotheses for high-temperature high-entropy alloys

Thank you for helping. This folder contains everything you need: no installation and no GitHub account. You only
need a web browser and a spreadsheet program (Excel, LibreOffice Calc or Google Sheets).

**Time needed:** about 4 hours in total, which you can split over several sessions. If you have less time, do
questions **Q01–Q10 only** (about 2 hours). Any set of completed questions is useful.

---

## Why we need you (1 minute)

We are testing whether AI systems can propose good research hypotheses for high-temperature high-entropy alloys.

1. We took recently published papers and turned the **introduction** of each into a research question. The
   question describes the problem but not what the paper did.
2. AI systems proposed a hypothesis for each question: what to change or study, why it should work, what should
   happen, and how to test it.
3. An **AI judge** then scored each hypothesis by comparing it with what the paper actually did.

All our conclusions rest on that AI judge. **Your ratings tell us whether the AI judge agrees with a materials
expert.** If it does not, our conclusions are not reliable. Nothing else we can do checks this.

---

## What you will do

- **20 research questions (Q01–Q20), each with 3 hypotheses labelled A, B and C: 60 hypotheses in total.**
- **Part 1, blind:** without knowing which paper a question came from:
  - rate each hypothesis for **plausibility**, **testability** and **usefulness** (1–5 each);
  - say whether the question makes sense;
  - guess which hypothesis is closest to what the authors actually did.
- **Part 2, after Part 1:** you see the paper (with a link to its free full text) and a summary of what it did and
  found. You then:
  - rate how closely each hypothesis **agrees with the paper** on four aspects (0–4 each);
  - check that our summary of the paper is accurate.

You enter all ratings in one spreadsheet with drop-down lists.

## Three important rules

1. **Finish Part 1 before you open Part 2.** Part 2 shows the paper and its results. Once you have seen them, you
   can no longer rate blindly.
2. **During Part 1, please do not search for the papers.**
3. **Please do not open other folders of this repository until you have sent your ratings back.** They contain the
   answers (which system wrote which hypothesis) and our own results, which could bias your ratings.

There are no right or wrong answers. We want your expert judgment, and please use the whole scale.
